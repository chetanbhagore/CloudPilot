| **Network Security** | **Malicious actor uses internal repo URL to probe intranet** | 28.6% | Critical | **Solved:** `validate_repository_url()` enforces DNS resolution and blocks loopback (`127.0.0.1`), RFC 1918, link-local, and AWS IMDS (`169.254.169.254`). |
